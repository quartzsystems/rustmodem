# +MS selects the modulation (V.250 clause 6.4.1): a carrier, automode,
# and four rates, each of which is 0, 300, 1200 or 2400. A subparameter
# left out stays as it is. B0 and B1 change the carrier to the one of
# the same rate in the other family: the ITU's V.21 and V.22, and Bell's
# 103 and 212A. S9 is the carrier response time and S10 the carrier loss
# time, in tenths of a second.

< ATE0\r\r\nOK\r\n
> AT+MS?\r
